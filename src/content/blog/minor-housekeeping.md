 I purchased **_MaxIm DL/CCD_** version 3.10 from Cyanogen Software, the top of the line image processing and CCD control software.  Haven't done anything with it yet except to work through the menus and try the scope control functions, which work wonderfully.  Once I can get some time to do a little bit of imaging, I hope to review this software.  Though not cheap, its versatility and easy of use could replace about 4 other programs that I am currently using.
