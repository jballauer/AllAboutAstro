Some days just aren't as enjoyable as others, and things like astronomy aren't a good recompense.

My grandmother ("Me-ma"), Gertie Ansley, passed away today at 4:40 PM. She was 96 years of age. All the family is doing well as this was somewhat expected, though your thoughts and prayers are always coveted.

Finalization of the arrangements are pending at the moment; however, the funeral will likely be held at Ash Creek Baptist Church, in Azle, Texas, on either Monday or Tuesday.

She was a remarkable woman...always a hero to me.
