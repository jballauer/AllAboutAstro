Well, I had gotten a little tired of the look of these pages, so I decided to do a slight redesign.  Not a whole lot has changed from the old format as far as content is concerned, but I do think you'll like the new look.  The following changes have been made:

*   Change of color scheme and navigation buttons.  All pages now have a more uniform theme.
*   Sub-headings at the top of each page give a summary of what is on the page.
*   Smaller fonts for a more crisp look; more useable for smaller screen resolutions.
*   Unified Gallery pages with sub-pages.

Take the new look for a spin and let me know what you think by emailing me at [jay@allaboutastro.com](mailto:jay@allaboutastro.com)
