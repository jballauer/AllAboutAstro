<h2>About this Image</h2>
<p>The region around Antares and Rho Ophiuchi is one of the most colorful in the sky -- glowing red and orange dust reflecting off nearby stars in shades of yellow and blue, all near the bright globular cluster M4. It rewards even short exposures because there's simply so much color and structure packed into a small area.</p>
<p>This was one of several simple, unattended data sets I shot for students at the workshop to practice their processing on -- about as close to "point and shoot" as astrophotography gets, and a good reminder of how much dark skies alone can do for you.</p>
