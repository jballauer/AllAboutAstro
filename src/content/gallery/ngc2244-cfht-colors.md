<p><strong>Processing Information:</strong> Calibration, registration, and DDP in CCDStack. Color mapping, cropping, color balance, levels/curves, sharpening, and noise removal (Astronomy Tools/Pro Digital Software) in Photoshop CS.</p>
<p><strong>Exposure Notes:</strong> SII data mapped to the red channel, Ha to green, and OIII to blue. Seeing was exceptional for the left side of the mosaic but poor for the right; interestingly, the left side came out much cleaner than the right despite having less exposure time in Ha.</p>

<h2>About this Image</h2>
<p>This is the same spectral-line data set as the Hubble-mapped shot, rearranged into the color scheme made known by the Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope, or CFHT. Whereas the Hubble mapping assigns Sulfur to red, Hydrogen to green, and Oxygen to blue, the CFHT mapping simply shifts the channels around: here, reddish areas represent hydrogen gases, greenish areas are oxygen, and bluish areas are sulfur. Mixing gases in the same region blends these into a distinct palette of intermediate colors.</p>
