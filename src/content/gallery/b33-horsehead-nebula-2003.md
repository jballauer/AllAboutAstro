An earlier attempt at [B33, the Horsehead Nebula](/gallery/b33-horsehead-nebula/), taken in 2003 with an SBIG ST-7E.

<div class="ke-two-col">
<div class="ke-two-col-specs">

<p><strong>Location:</strong> Ballauer Observatory in Azle, Texas</p>
<p><strong>Seeing:</strong> 8/10 (2.5 FWHM)</p>
<p><strong>Transparency:</strong> 4/10</p>
<p><strong>Date and Time:</strong> November 18, 2003 @ 11:00PM</p>
<p><strong>Equipment:</strong> SBIG ST-7E, Tak FSQ-106 @ f/5, and Celestron CGE mount</p>
<p><strong>Length:</strong> Five 20 minute exposures binned 1x1</p>
<p><strong>Processing Information:</strong> Dark frames, gradient removal, registration and Sigma Combine in MaxIm 3.0. Levels, Curves, Gaussian Blur and Unsharp Mask in Photoshop 7.</p>
<p><strong>Exposure Notes:</strong> First light image with the new Celestron CGE mount.</p>

</div>
<div class="ke-sidebar ke-two-col-about" data-color="teal">

<h2>About this Image</h2>
<p>This is an earlier attempt at B33, the Horsehead Nebula, shot two years before the <a href="/gallery/b33-horsehead-nebula/">2005 image</a> using the SBIG ST-7E camera instead of the later STL-11000M.</p>
