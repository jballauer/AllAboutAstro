An earlier attempt at [IC 1805, the Heart Nebula](/gallery/ic1805-heart-nebula/), taken in 2004 with a Takahashi FSQ-106 and SBIG STL-6303E.

<div class="ke-two-col">
<div class="ke-two-col-specs">

<p><strong>Location:</strong> The Ballauer Observatory near Azle, Texas</p>
<p><strong>Date:</strong> September 8, 2004</p>
<p><strong>Temperature:</strong> 60 degrees F</p>
<p><strong>Seeing:</strong> 8/10, steady</p>
<p><strong>Transparency:</strong> 8/10</p>
<p><strong>Scope/Mount:</strong> Takahashi FSQ-106 @ f/5 and Tak NJP mount</p>
<p><strong>Camera:</strong> SBIG STL-6303E, self-guided</p>
<p><strong>Filter:</strong> Custom Scientific 4.5nm Hydrogen-Alpha</p>
<p><strong>Exposure Info:</strong> HaRGB image - 150:20:20:20 minutes (15 min. subexposures for luminance; 5 min. subexposures for RGB, binned 2x2)</p>
<p><strong>Processing Info:</strong> Dark frame calibration, flat-fields, de-blooming, registration, and average combine of all channels in MaxIm 4.0. Digital development in MaxIm. Blending of final data, levels, curves, unsharp mask, despeckle, and color balance in Photoshop CS.</p>
<p><strong>Extra notes:</strong> The red channel is blended 50/50 with the Ha luminance. Full luminance used on final combine. Special thanks to Three Rivers Foundation (3RF) for the use of some of the equipment used in creating this image.</p>

<h2>About this Image</h2>
<p>This is an earlier attempt at IC 1805, the Heart Nebula, shot two years before the <a href="/gallery/ic1805-heart-nebula/">2006 image</a> using the SBIG STL-6303E camera instead of the later STL-11000M.</p>
