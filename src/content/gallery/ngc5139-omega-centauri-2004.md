<h2>About this Object</h2>
<p>One of the good things about living in Texas is that we can see this gem just above our horizon. Perhaps the grandest globular cluster of them all, Omega Centauri is compact and slightly oval in shape, shining at a naked eye brightness of 4th magnitude with an angular size that approximates our own moon at full phase. Millions and millions of stars comprise this beauty of an object which is easily resolved by even small aperture scopes, that is as long as the object is high enough at Northern latitudes to avoid atmospheric interference.</p>
