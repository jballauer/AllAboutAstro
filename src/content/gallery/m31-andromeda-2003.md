An earlier attempt at M31, the Andromeda Galaxy — see the [current version](/gallery/m31-andromeda-galaxy/) for the full writeup on this object.

<div class="ke-two-col">
<div class="ke-two-col-specs">

<p><strong>Location:</strong> Ballauer Observatory in Azle, Texas</p>
<p><strong>Date and Time:</strong> December 30, 2003 @ 11:00PM</p>
<p><strong>Seeing:</strong> 8/10 (1.4 FWHM)</p>
<p><strong>Transparency:</strong> 3/10</p>
<p><strong>Equipment:</strong> Tak FSQ-106 @ f/5, and Celestron CGE mount</p>
<p><strong>Camera:</strong> SBIG ST-7E with CFW-8a filter wheel, SBIG standard filters</p>
<p><strong>Exposure Info:</strong> LRGB image (7 x 10 min L, 3 x 5 min R, 3 x 5 min G, 7 x 5 min B)</p>
<p><strong>Processing Information:</strong> MaxIm (Darks, align/combine, and digital development), Photoshop 7 (Levels, Curves, Blur/Sharpening, masking and blending, color balance)</p>
<p><strong>Other exposure info:</strong> This shot was taken with a string taped over the aperture end of the scope in a cross pattern. This creates the four-point diffraction pattern on the brighter stars in the image. It's just a personal preference.</p>
